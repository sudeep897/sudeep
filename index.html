Our Story

Building Nepal's Digital Future

Supeksha Innovations Nepal was founded with a simple but powerful vision: to help Nepali businesses, schools, and organizations harness the full power of technology — right here, locally, with deep understanding of the market.

From Kathmandu's Thamel to the hills of Pokhara, our software runs schools, powers shops, and connects communities. We believe technology should be accessible, meaningful, and transformative for every Nepali enterprise.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take? +
Project timelines vary by scope. A landing page takes 1–2 weeks. A full web application or mobile app typically takes 6–16 weeks. Our School ERP implementation takes 8–12 weeks. We always provide a detailed timeline before starting.
Do you work with clients outside Kathmandu? +
Absolutely! We work with clients across Nepal — Pokhara, Biratnagar, Chitwan, and beyond. We also serve international clients. All communication can be done remotely via Zoom, Google Meet, or WhatsApp.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept bank transfer, eSewa, Khalti, FonePay, and international payments via Wise or PayPal. We typically work on a 50% advance, 25% at midpoint, and 25% at delivery model.
Do you provide hosting and domain registration? +
Yes! We offer end-to-end services including domain registration, hosting setup on AWS or DigitalOcean, SSL certificates, and ongoing server management. We can also work with your existing hosting provider.
What post-launch support do you offer? +
All projects include a free bug-fix warranty period (30–90 days depending on scope). Beyond that, we offer monthly maintenance packages starting at NPR 5,000/month covering updates, security patches, and priority support.

💳 Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. General Policy

Due to the custom nature of our digital services, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. We are committed to client satisfaction and will work to resolve any issues before processing refunds.

2. Before Project Start

If you cancel before work begins, 100% of the advance payment is refunded minus a NPR 2,000 processing fee.

3. During Development

If you cancel mid-project, you are billed for completed work at our hourly rate (NPR 1,500–3,000/hour). Any remaining advance after this calculation is refunded.

No refunds are issued after project delivery and client approval. However, we provide a warranty period for bug fixes at no charge.
